# J4 Energy Storage – Norway: references and GenAI declarations

Public page for our J4 group project (Trinity College Dublin, 2026). The QR code on our poster and video links here.

## Adding your story

Each story has one file in `stories/`. Replace the placeholder with your own content, using `TEMPLATE.md` as the layout:

1. **References**: numbered to match the superscripts on your slides, each with a link and what you used it for.
2. **GenAI declaration**: one row per project activity (what GenAI did, your role, how you checked it), plus a table of GenAI errors that were caught and corrected.

To edit on github.com: open your file in `stories/`, click the pencil icon, paste your content, then click **Commit changes**. The website updates within a minute or two.
