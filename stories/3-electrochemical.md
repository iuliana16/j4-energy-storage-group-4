# Story 3: Electrochemical – lithium iron phosphate battery at Selsøyvær

**Author:** Diarmuid Moriarty · **Site:** Kvarøy Fiskeoppdrett's Selsøyvær salmon farm, Rødøy, Nordland, Norway; off-grid (no price zone), more than 4 km from shore power · **System:** 273 kWh / 125 kW lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery in a 20 ft container on the feed barge, charged by the diesel generator in 4 short runs a day

**Own model (not from a source):** the winter and summer load profiles (15–90 kW, about 640 kWh/day); the minute-by-minute generator and battery simulation (4 generator runs a day, about 7 h of running instead of 24 h, so −71%); storage need of 142 kWh usable, 85 kW charging and about 5 h between generator runs; the 273 kWh sizing (4 strings × 22 modules, 845 V) with a 125 kW inverter; 218 kWh usable when new and 175 kWh at end of life; charging rate 0.31 C; about 505 cycles a year and a battery replacement at year 12; payback 2.8–5.3 years, NPV +€0.18–0.62 M and storage cost €0.30/kWh (levelised cost of storage); 27 g CO₂e per kWh delivered, 82% of it from battery manufacture; diesel saved 102–203 t CO₂e a year; carbon payback 3.6–7 months; the verdict that the system stays profitable in all cases tested except ship-grade costs with low fuel savings. All costs in 2026 EUR, 7% discount rate, 20 years.
